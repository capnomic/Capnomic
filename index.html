Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how information is handled when you use Capnomic Financial.

App: Capnomic Financial Package ID: com.capnomic.financial Effective: November 3, 2026

This policy applies to the Capnomic Financial Android app. Please read it alongside any notices shown within the app or by a connected third-party service.

01 Introduction

Capnomic Financial (“the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a financial calculation and planning application published by Capnomic. It provides tools such as loan and EMI calculations, deposit estimates, investment projections, goal planning, and retirement planning.

The app’s calculations are for informational and educational purposes only. They are not a loan approval, an offer of financial products, or personalized financial advice.

02 Information You Enter

You may enter figures such as loan amounts, interest rates, income, expenses, savings, investment assumptions, or other values needed to use a calculator or planning feature.

Do not enter passwords, card security codes, or other authentication credentials into the app.

Information may be processed on your device to calculate results or support features you choose to use. If a feature sends information to an external service, that processing should be described in the relevant feature and in the app’s applicable disclosures.

03 Saved Information

If you use a feature that saves calculations or plans, the app may store the information needed to make those items available to you later. The location and duration of storage depend on how that feature is implemented.

You can remove information stored locally by clearing the app’s data through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app. Information handled by an external service may be subject to that service’s retention and deletion practices.

04 Sharing and Third Parties

If you choose to share a calculation or report, the app may pass the content you selected to Android’s sharing interface. The app does not control how the destination app or service uses information you choose to send to it.

Some features may rely on third-party services, software libraries, or APIs. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. The app’s Google Play disclosures should reflect any collection or sharing carried out by the app or its integrated third-party components.

05 Permissions and Device Data

The app may use Android permissions or device features needed for specific functions. The permissions requested by the installed version can be reviewed in your Android settings and on the app’s Google Play listing.

We will keep this policy and the app’s Google Play disclosures consistent with the app’s actual permissions, integrations, and data practices.

06 Children’s Privacy

Capnomic Financial is a general-purpose financial utility and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature of the app, please contact us using the details below so we can review the matter.

07 Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the app’s design to protect information handled by our services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information stored on your device is also subject to your device’s security settings.

Retention periods depend on where information is stored and the feature involved. Locally stored information can generally be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Third-party services follow their own retention practices.

08 Your Choices

You decide what information to enter and whether to use optional sharing or connected features. You can stop using a feature at any time. For information handled by an external service, please consult that service’s privacy policy.

09 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Please review it periodically.
