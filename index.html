1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Capnomic Financial (“we”, “our”, or “the Application”), developed by [Insert Developer or Organization Name], handles your information when you use our mobile application on Android.

Capnomic Financial provides offline-first financial calculation and planning tools, including Loan EMI calculations, Fixed Deposit estimates, SIP and investment projections, and retirement planning. We respect your privacy and believe you should know exactly what data the application touches and where it is kept.

2. Information and Data Collected

We do not collect, harvest, or transmit your personal or financial information to our own servers.

  • No Account Creation: The application does not require registration, login, username, password, or profile creation.
  • No Identity or Financial Profiling: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, bank account details, credit card numbers, or government identification numbers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The application does not integrate analytics tools, advertising networks, device tracking libraries, or telemetry software.

3. On-Device (Local) Data Storage

The application operates locally on your mobile device. Any figures you input (such as principal amounts, interest rates, tenure lengths) and any saved plans or comparison profiles you create are stored exclusively on your device using Android’s sandboxed application private storage (SharedPreferences).

  • Your saved calculations remain on your device and are accessible only by the Capnomic Financial application.
  • We have no access to the calculations, plans, or values you save.
  • You can delete this locally stored data at any time by clearing the application storage via your device settings (Settings > Apps > Capnomic Financial > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Transfer

User-Initiated Sharing: Data leaves your device only when you deliberately decide to share it using your device’s built-in Android share sheet.

The application features optional “Share” buttons on calculation screens (such as loan breakdowns, fixed deposit summaries, and comparison tables). When you tap this button:

  • The application generates a plain text summary of your calculation.
  • The summary is passed directly to the standard Android system sharing interface (Intent.ACTION_SEND).
  • You choose which external app (e.g., messaging apps, email clients, or note-taking apps) receives the text.

Once transmitted through the Android sharing dialog, that text is processed according to the privacy policy of the destination service or application you selected. Capnomic Financial does not monitor, log, or store your sharing history.

5. Device Permissions

Capnomic Financial is designed to require minimal device access:

  • No Sensitive Permissions: The application does not request or require access to your location, camera, microphone, device contacts, call logs, SMS, or media libraries.
  • No Network Permissions: The application’s manifest does not declare or request the Android INTERNET permission. The application does not establish network connections.

6. Children’s Privacy

Capnomic Financial is a general-purpose utility designed for financial planning and calculations. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the application does not collect personal data, transmit information to remote servers, or create user accounts, we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Because we do not operate backend servers or transfer your data across the internet, your financial inputs and calculations cannot be accessed, intercepted, or breached on our servers. Your data security relies on the standard security protections of your Android device, including your lock screen, encryption, and device operating system controls.
